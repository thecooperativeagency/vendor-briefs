Website defaults & UX issues

Discussed unexpected defaults (payment estimate and saved zip), the missing initial zip popover, and whether site should auto-fill based on saved cookies. Participants noticed the site defaulting to a previously saved ZIP and showing the payment estimate without an obvious way to opt out. The historical behavior (a popover prompting ZIP on first entry) has changed. The group debated whether the behavior is caused by retained cookies/cache and whether it should be corrected or made optional. An escalation email will be sent to address the flaw.

InsightSite is defaulting ZIP/payment estimate based on saved data; previous popover behavior removed.
RiskPotential cookie/cache interaction causing unexpected defaults.
ActionSend email to escalate and decide ownership.
TaskInvestigate zip/default behavior — Engineering/product to confirm source (cookies, saved profile, or code change) and propose fix or opt-out for payment estimate and zip defaulting.

Mystery-shop lead delivery & IP rate limits

Mystery shop revealed IP-based submission limits caused many dealers to miss leads; the team needs a workaround and to redo the test. The team preloaded unique phone numbers and submitted many site leads simultaneously. New IP protocols limited submissions per IP within short windows, which caused two-thirds of dealers to not receive the leads. This discovery invalidates the initial mystery-shop results and requires a re-run after implementing a distributed submission approach or coordinating with platform vendors.

Pain pointIP-rate-limits blocked bulk mystery-shop submissions; many dealers missed leads.
ActionNeed to redo mystery shop after technical workaround.
TaskPlan mystery shop re-run — Create an execution plan that avoids IP limits (stagger submissions, use distributed IPs, or coordinate with vendor) and schedule the redo.

Lead response practices

Emphasis that texting and calling outperform email; best-practice is to send multiple contact channels quickly and add CRM notes. Team agreed most customers respond by text or phone, and email is low-performing. Recommended practice: attempt text first, call if no response, and send email only when needed; always add notes to CRM and retry communication via multiple channels within a short window.

InsightTexting yields better conversion than email; send multi-channel follow-ups quickly.
ActionAdd contact attempts as CRM notes and re-attempt outreach.
