🧠 Key Points

Website defaults & UX issues The site appears to default to a saved ZIP and shows a payment estimate without an obvious opt-out. The old ZIP popover behavior seems gone. Likely cookie/cache or saved-profile behavior; needs an escalation email and a real fix or opt-out.
Mystery-shop lead delivery IP-based submission limits blocked many leads from reaching dealers. The original test results are not reliable until the shop is rerun with a distributed submission approach or other workaround.
Lead response practices Text and phone beat email. Best practice is text first, call if needed, and log every contact attempt in the CRM.
Reporting & metrics The conversion report was removed from PPN; the team pulled separate YTD data and reported a 74% win rate and a 9/10 website score.
Inventory photos & V Auto integration Photo ordering broke and required manual re-sorting. V Auto transfers are still not trusted to preserve categories correctly, so manual QC remains necessary.
