Working take — Podium swap candidate

VinSolutions VCA is an agentic sales assistant that lives inside the CRM you already run. It texts/emails customers, keeps multi-topic conversations going, books test drives and appraisals, writes appointments and tasks onto the salesperson’s CRM record, and backs off when a human has already touched the lead. Today’s channels are text + email (web chat and voice are roadmap). If BHAG’s goal is fewer third-party chat silos and tighter VinSolutions lead handling, this is the cleanest native path we have seen — at a stated $700/store/mo, no setup fee, 30-day out.

What it is

VCA (shown in product UI as Vinessa / VCA Virtual Contact Assistant) is VinSolutions’ next-gen assistant — positioned against their older NLP bot. Instead of rigid scripts, it runs goal-directed sales conversations: keep context, pull inventory facts, firm up vague times against store hours, schedule the appointment, remind the customer what to bring, and escalate pricing/finance to a human with a CRM task.

It reads recent communications (about the last 20 messages / 30 days), uses the inventory feed for year/make/model/trim/features/availability, and can present as a named human-style agent or identify as a virtual assistant — dealer-controlled.

Feature → benefit (GM lens)

Agentic multi-topic conversation
Benefit: Handles hours, vehicle questions, trade, and appointment in one thread — fewer dropped internet leads waiting on a BDC seat.
Primary goal = book the appointment
Benefit: Optimizes for showroom traffic (test drive + appraisal), not endless chat that never lands on the calendar.
Writes appts, test drives, appraisals into CRM
Benefit: Salesperson sees the appointment on the customer record under their name — no dual-system copy/paste from Podium.
Inventory-aware answers
Benefit: Confirms availability and equipment from the feed so the bot doesn’t invent cars you don’t have.
Reads last ~20 messages / 30 days
Benefit: Continuity after human handoffs — customer doesn’t re-explain the whole story every touch.
Process include/exclude + cadence buffers
Benefit: Turn VCA on for new leads, after-hours, KBB ICO, aging — and keep a human-first window during business hours if you want.
Suppression on recent human contact
Benefit: Stops AI pile-on when a rep already texted/called — protects customer experience and CSI.
Pricing / finance → human task
Benefit: Bot does not freestyle deals. Pushback on price creates a salesperson task instead of a bad number in writing.
Tone, greeting, differentiator, hours windows
Benefit: Brand-safe customization (friendly vs formal, name vs generic, store story) without a custom chatbot project.
Native VinSolutions surface
Benefit: Same login managers already use. Engagement state (“Not engaged” / conversation banner) sits on the customer dashboard.

Skills the assistant ships with

  1. Confirm vehicle availability
  2. Provide basic vehicle info (Y/M/M, miles, MPG, type, trim, features)
  3. Schedule appointments
  4. Trade-in questions
  5. Collect trade-in info & update customer profile
  6. Provide dealership info (hours, address)
  7. Get staff involved when needed
  8. Remind customers what to bring (DL, title, insurance)
