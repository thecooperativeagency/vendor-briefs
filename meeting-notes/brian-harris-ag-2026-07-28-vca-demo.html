What was demonstrated

VinSolutions presented an agentic virtual assistant that runs sales conversations and writes results into the CRM. Presenter contrasted it with VinSolutions’ older NLP-based system.

Stated behaviors during the demo:

  • Conducts multi-topic sales conversations (hours, vehicle interest, trade, appointment)
  • Primary goal described as scheduling test drives / in-person appointments
  • Gathers trade and vehicle interest details
  • Creates appointments, test-drive, and appraisal entries in CRM under the salesperson and consumer profile
  • Reads recent communications (about the last 20 messages in 30 days)
  • Pulls inventory details (year, make, model, trim, features) and availability from the inventory feed
  • Creates tasks or appointments in CRM when needed
  • Can present as human-style or identify as a virtual assistant; can be named (dealer-controlled)
  • Escalates pricing/finance discussion to a human and creates a task for the assigned salesperson

Feature → what it does (from the demo)

Agentic multi-topic conversation
Handles store hours, vehicle questions, trade interest, and appointment scheduling in one thread.
Appointment-focused goal
Demo conversation ended with a booked test drive and appraisal on a specific unit and time.
CRM write-back
Creates appointments, test-drive, and appraisal records in VinSolutions under the assigned salesperson.
Inventory feed
Answers with year/make/model/trim/features and availability from the inventory feed.
Conversation context
Reads roughly the last 20 messages within 30 days for continuity.
Process include / exclude
Dealer chooses which CRM processes trigger the assistant (e.g. new leads, KBB ICO, after-hours, aging).
Cadence and buffers
Configurable timing (example shown: delayed re-engage; optional human-first window such as 5 minutes during business hours).
Suppression on human contact
Checks suppression events; disengages if a human contacted the consumer within a configured period.
Pricing / finance handoff
Does not complete price negotiation in the demo thread; creates a task for the salesperson and keeps the appointment.
Identity and tone controls
Settings shown: greeting by name, tone of voice, store differentiator text, email sign-off, texting time window, site/finance URLs.

Skills listed on the deck

From the VinSolutions slide “Virtual Assistant: Skills”:

  1. Confirm vehicle availability for customers
  2. Provide basic vehicle info (year, make, model, miles, MPG, type, trim, features)
  3. Schedule appointments for customers
  4. Trade-in questions
  5. Collect trade-in info and update customer profile
  6. Provide dealership info (hours, address)
  7. Get dealership staff involved when needed
  8. Remind customers what to bring for appointments (DL, title, insurance)
  9. Determine customer sentiment — requires Predictive Insights

Process control (when it runs)

Slide titled “Virtual Assistant: You control when it will reach out via processes” listed:

  1. New sales leads
  2. New KBB ICO leads
  3. New lead received after hours
  4. After a visit has ended
  5. Aging leads
  6. Schedule appointments and send appointment reminders
  7. Reschedule missed appointments
  8. Cancel existing appointments
  9. Sold delivered (goal: set service appointment — shown grey on the slide)
